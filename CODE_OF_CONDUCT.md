# How we treat each other

We follow the GNU Kind Communications Guidelines: <https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/kind-communication.html>.

In short: assume good faith, be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people working in
a second language or with assistive technology; criticise ideas, not people; and stay on topic.

If someone's conduct here worries you, write to corwin@bru.st. A person will answer, and we will not
share what you tell us without asking you first.
